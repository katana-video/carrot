# Carrot IR — worked examples

63 natural-language requests and the IR documents a compiler
produced for them, every one re-validated by `plan()` against the listed
attachment facts at generation time (so what you read here is exactly what the
engine accepts). Normative rules: [ir-spec.md](./ir-spec.md); machine
schema: [schema/0.1/document.json](./schema/0.1/document.json).

Conventions: asset ids are opaque and chosen by the author (the attachment
list maps them to files); `placementSource`/`regionSource` record whether a
placement was stated by the user or defaulted (the app lets people drag
defaulted ones); `output.name` is the deliverable's base filename.

Generated by `experiments/nl-compile/gen-examples.mts` in the development
monorepo from an eval run — do not edit by hand.
